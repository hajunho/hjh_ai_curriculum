# Lecture 07 — Business Machine Learning in Practice

> This lecture takes the models you learned in lecture06 and turns them into "projects that actually run inside a company."
> We cover the whole journey: framing the problem, designing features, validating properly, and the final step of turning a model's answers into decisions that make money.

## What you will learn in this lecture

- How a data project actually flows from start to finish, and the failure patterns that doomed projects share
- How to translate "recall improved by 1 percentage point" into "this much revenue per year" — the language for talking to executives
- A workflow for doing exploratory data analysis (EDA) as a procedure, not a gut feeling
- Feature engineering, categorical encoding, scaling and pipelines — the prep work that shapes performance more than the model does
- Cross-validation and imbalanced-data handling — the techniques that make performance numbers *trustworthy*
- The practical trio: sales demand forecasting, customer churn prediction, and fraud detection
- How to feed model probabilities into cost-benefit math and extract the optimal decision (who gets the coupon?)

## Where this lecture shows up on the job

- **Quarterly business planning**: forecast next week's and next month's revenue including day-of-week and seasonal effects, and plan inventory and staffing (level08)
- **CRM campaigns**: produce a ranked list of at-risk customers with a recommended action for each, and hand it to the marketing team (level09, 11)
- **Risk and audit teams**: design a fraud-alert threshold that fits the team's daily alert-handling capacity (its budget) (level07, 10)
- **Working with (or auditing) a data team**: the questions that expose data leakage and single-split traps hiding behind a report that claims "95% accuracy" (level03–06)
- **Executive reporting**: instead of showing a confusion matrix, say "this model can prevent about 400 million KRW a year in churn losses" (level01, 11)

## Tips for working through it

- Every level is designed to be read while you run `main.py` yourself. Check that your numbers match the notes (seeds are fixed).
- To run: use the virtual environment at the repo root, `python3 main.py` (see the root `SETUP.md` for environment setup)
- All data is synthetically generated by `common/hjh_data.py` (cafe sales, subscription churn, card transactions). No internet connection needed.
- level00–07 are the parts; level08–11 are the finished products. If you skipped a parts level and get stuck in a case study, loop back.
